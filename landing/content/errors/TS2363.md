---
code: TS2363
summary: The right operand of `-`, `*`, `/`, `%` or `**` isn't a number, bigint or enum. Convert it first.
---

## What it means

Arithmetic operators other than `+` need numeric operands. A string, boolean or object on
the right is reported.

## Example

```ts example
const count = "3";

export const total = 10 * count;
```

## How to fix

- Convert explicitly: `10 * Number(count)`.
