## What it means

Each `parse<T>` call generates validator code that ends up in your bundle. A validator over the budget (8 KB by default, `[sound.guard] max-validator-kb`) is reported so large validators don't slip into bundles unnoticed.

## How to fix

- Validate a smaller type, for example the part of a response you actually use (`Pick<T, ...>`).
- Or raise the budget: `[sound.guard] max-validator-kb` in `ts-r.toml`.

Sound mode is defined in [design record 0007](../../../docs/design/0007-sound-mode.md) (accepted 2026-10-15).
